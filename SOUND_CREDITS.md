# Sound credits

The built-in tier sounds in `app/src/main/res/raw/` were taken from free, openly licensed packages on npm,
then trimmed, loudness-normalised and converted to mono Ogg Vorbis.

| File | Tier | Source | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sfx_horn.ogg` | 🔵 must-take | [`mlg-horn`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/mlg-horn) `mlg-airhorn.mp3` (© 2023 Joaquim da Rosa) | MIT |
| `sfx_coin.ogg` | 🟢 good | [`uisfx`](https://github.com/romainsimon/uisfx) `sounds/arcade/reward.mp3` | CC0 1.0 |
| `sfx_ready.ogg` | 🟡 meets target | [Kenney](https://www.kenney.nl) "Voiceover Pack" `Audio (Male)/ready.ogg` | CC0 1.0 |
| `sfx_poop.ogg` | 🔴 below target | [`terminalfart`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/terminalfart) `sounds/wet.mp3` (© 2026 Electric Wolfe Marshmallow Hypertext) | Apache-2.0 |

The packages do not document where the original recordings came from. If you redistribute the app,
check the upstream projects or replace the clips with your own (Settings → Sounds → Change file).
